## 01 · Overview

**Four brands. Four customers. One loyalty framework.**

At Grupo Salinas I designed loyalty systems for Elektra, Banco Azteca, ITALIKA, and Presta Prenda. Each brand had its own voice, customer, and business rules. The challenge was making the program feel native to each one without reinventing the wheel four times.

---

## 02 · Challenge

**Four brands, four distinct customers, one loyalty program.**

Without a shared architecture, the result would have been four fragmented products: higher cost, more inconsistency, and a worse experience for users who cross the ecosystem. We had to respect each brand's identity without giving up proven usability patterns.

---

## 03 · Approach

**A shared interaction architecture, distinct brand skins.**

I defined a framework of flows, components, and loyalty rules that could adapt to each identity. Operational consistency lived underneath; brand expression lived on top. That let teams work in parallel while keeping onboarding, points, redemptions, and messaging coherent.

---

## 04 · Outcome

**4 parallel systems on one foundation.**

The program scaled across the four brands with differentiated identities and a shared interaction base: less rework, more clarity for product and engineering, and a loyalty experience recognizable within the Salinas ecosystem.
