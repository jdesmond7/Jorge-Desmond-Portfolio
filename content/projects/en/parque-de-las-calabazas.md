## 01 · Overview

**A seasonal theme park needed a digital presence that felt like the visit — and that Google could still read.**

Parque de las Calabazas is an intense physical experience: atmosphere, routes, photography, ticket sales. The site had to carry that feeling onto the screen without becoming a “pretty but invisible” microsite.

---

## 02 · Challenge

**An immersive site usually sacrifices SEO. This one had to do both.**

The tension was clear: effects, visual narrative, and high-impact storytelling versus indexing, speed, content structure, and conversion to purchase. Design couldn't pick only one side.

---

## 03 · Approach

**I designed the experience in layers: atmosphere first, semantics always.**

The interface prioritizes an immersive entry, but critical content — dates, location, pricing, ticket CTAs — lives in a structure readable for users and search engines. The handoff to code treated performance and SEO as day-one requirements.

---

## 04 · Outcome

**A site that sells the experience and also gets found.**

The result is a web presence with strong brand personality, a clear visual journey, and explicit conversion paths — without giving up the technical foundation a seasonal product needs to be discovered.
