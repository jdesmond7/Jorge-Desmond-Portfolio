I'm jorge. Mexican, based in Monterrey. I train calisthenics. I draw when I can.

I design systems, the kind that keep products from breaking as they grow. Right now I'm a Senior Product Designer at [GroWrk Remote](https://growrk.com/), where I also wear the AI Design Architect hat. On the side I teach design at [Tec de Monterrey](https://tec.mx/) and I'm building [Prowell](https://prowell.app/), a coaching platform for wellness trainers.

The last five years have been design systems, tokens, components, and decisions that look simple but have three layers underneath. I also write my own designs in code when the project calls for it.

Before this I was at [SPIN by OXXO](https://spin.mx/) and [Grupo Salinas](https://www.gruposalinas.com/). I learned that design without business context is decoration.

At night I illustrate. I prefer analog: india ink, alcohol markers, paper. When the project calls for it, I work digital too, in Procreate. No Ctrl+Z if I can help it.

I'm building three brands, slowly and on purpose: desmond.design for systems consulting, [Built by Des](https://builtbydes.com/) for fitness, and [Frame & Font](https://frameandfont.com/) for design education (this last one with my partner, in work and life, Liz).

Write me at [hola@jorgedesmond.com](mailto:hola@jorgedesmond.com) or find me on [LinkedIn](https://linkedin.com/in/jorgedesmond) / [Instagram](https://instagram.com/jorgedesmond).

Cheers, jorge
